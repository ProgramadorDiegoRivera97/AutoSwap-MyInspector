  • Sound-producing devices other than the horn are installed

  • Brake fluid reservoir cap is missing

  • One or more shock absorbers are missing
  • Suspension bump stops are missing or in poor condition
  • Urban taxi meter is missing (taxis)

  • Stabilizer bar is improperly secured
  • Suspension ball-joint dust boots are missing or torn
  • Excessive play or improper attachment in any steering component
  • Hydraulic steering system leaks without continuous dripping
  • Hydraulic steering system leaks with continuous dripping

  • Transmission oil leak with continuous dripping
